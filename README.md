## The review

> One star. Gravity has held everything down for billions of years and still will not let me reach the top shelf.

111 characters, one line, deadpan throughout: the complaint is read straight,
and the sting lands in the last few words.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the review to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`) and asserts it is a
single line of 1–160 characters. Run it twice and the output is
byte-identical.

## Why gravity

The sun gets blamed for the heat, Mondays for the week, sleep for the
hours — but gravity is the essential nobody files against, even though
it is the one standing between every short person and the top shelf.
A grievance billions of years in the making, still unresolved.
